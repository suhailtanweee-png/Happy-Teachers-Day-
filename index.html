A Letter From Us

We knew you as a teacher. But we will remember you as something more.

You were Xenodochial before we knew the word. You never made any of us feel like a stranger. Whether first bench or last bench, you welcomed every doubt with same smile.

You taught Biosciences, but you taught us life. It was never just about diagrams. You made us see how a small cell holds a whole universe. M.Sc. B.Ed is on your bio, but your real degree is patience.

From JMI to HNB GU to KUK, from Delhi to Assam. Your journey itself was a lesson. You brought Delhi's big dreams to our small town Hojai, and made us dream bigger.

This is our last Teacher's Day in this uniform, Sir. As outgoing batch of Class 12, we are about to step out. We will forget many formulas, but not how you taught us to be kind, curious, welcoming.

Thank you for being our guide.

With love, always.
Outgoing Batch of Class 12 (2025-26)
